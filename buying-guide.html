A little care
for your skincare.

How should I store my products?+
Follow the storage directions on the packaging and replace caps after use. Keep sunscreen away from excessive heat and direct sunlight, including a hot car. Read sunscreen storage guidance (opens in a new tab).
What is the expiry date or period after opening?+
Batch dates vary. Check the bottle and box for an expiry date and an open-jar symbol if present. Ask the seller for the batch currently shipping; we cannot assign one expiry date to every order.
Can I use skincare alongside retinol, vitamin C or a prescription?+
Check the directions of every product in your routine. Introduce one new step at a time and ask your dermatologist or pharmacist about a prescription or an ingredient concern. Our routine finder does not assess medical compatibility.
What payment methods can I use?+
Shopee offers options such as cards and supported digital payment methods. Availability depends on your account, device and order. Choose from the options shown at checkout. See Shopee payment options (opens in a new tab).
Do I need the Shopee app or an account?+
You can browse Aureveil without signing in. Following a purchase link opens Shopee, where sign-in may be required to complete an order. A link may open the app if it is installed. Keep the order in the account you will use for support.
