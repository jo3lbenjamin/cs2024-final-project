Simple things to know

A closer look at your mist.

How do I use Cloud Mist?+
Hold 20–30cm from the face and spray once or twice, then allow it to absorb. Follow the current packaging instructions.
Is Cloud Mist a makeup setting spray?+
Cloud Mist is for hydration, refreshing and a dewy finish. It is not a makeup-fixing spray and does not promise longer makeup wear.
Does it provide sun protection?+
No SPF is stated for Cloud Mist. Use a labelled sunscreen for sun protection and follow its reapplication directions.
