Simple things to know

A closer look at your mist.

How do I use Cloud Mist?+
Hold 20–30cm from the face and spray once or twice, then allow it to absorb. Follow the current packaging instructions.
Is Cloud Mist a makeup setting spray?+
Cloud Mist is for hydration, refreshing and a dewy finish. It is not a makeup-fixing spray and does not promise longer makeup wear.
Does it provide sun protection?+
No SPF is stated for Cloud Mist. Use a labelled sunscreen for sun protection and follow its reapplication directions.

Before you choose

Your questions.
A clearer next step.

Will it suit oily or sensitive skin?+
The fine spray is described with a dewy finish. If you prefer a matte look, keep that finish in mind. Review the full ingredient list for ingredients you avoid and introduce it carefully; individual reviews are not a guarantee for your skin.
Can I use it with makeup?+
Cloud Mist is a hydration step and does not promise longer makeup wear. For morning skincare, use it before sunscreen and makeup. Avoid treating a facial mist as sunscreen reapplication.
Does it replace a serum or moisturiser?+
It is an optional hydration step. Keep your usual moisturiser if your skin needs it; you do not need to replace a comfortable routine just to add a mist.
How long will one bottle last?+
The 120ml bottle’s lifespan depends on the amount and frequency you use. Use the packaging’s spray directions rather than a fixed day-by-day allowance.
How do I check expiry and storage?+
Read the expiry date and any open-jar period-after-opening symbol on the bottle or box. Ask the seller for the current batch details before ordering. Follow the storage directions and replace the cap after use. Explore product care.
How do delivery and returns work?+
Your order is placed on Shopee. Check the delivery estimate and fees for your address before payment, and read the applicable return conditions. Our shipping guide and returns guide explain where to find them.
