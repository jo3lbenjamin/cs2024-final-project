A pairing, with purpose

A ritual you can
make your own.

This pairing brings together two complementary roles. Layering the products does not provide extra labelled UV protection. The Daily Sun Serum supplies the labelled SPF; the mist supplies hydration.

Start with your preferred skincare, finish with sunscreen according to the label, and use makeup afterwards if you choose. A mist is not a substitute for sunscreen reapplication.
