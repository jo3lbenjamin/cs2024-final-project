HUMAN SPF TEST · MANUFACTURER SUMMARY

50.5

Mean measured SPF

ISO 24444:2019 testing in 10 adults, applied to the back at 2.0 mg/cm². This is a UVB sunburn-protection measurement, rather than a wrinkle-reduction result.

INSTRUMENT-MEASURED HYDRATION

20 adults

Surface moisture, measured

Mean forearm readings: 47.45 A.U. before application, 80.52 A.U. immediately after, and 79.21 A.U. after 30 minutes in 20–25% humidity. A.U. are instrument units.

Read a result with its method

The manufacturer identifies ProbeM Skin Research Center, Korea, for the April 2023 moisture studies. The low-humidity study measured forearms indoors and excluded people with sensitive skin. Its results describe surface hydration; they do not establish facial wrinkle reduction, acne treatment or all-day UV protection.

What research tells us.

Human studies help explain why ingredients are used. Their findings belong to the tested preparations, concentrations and timescales.

Centella and three forms of hyaluronic acid

The ingredient reference for Daily Sun Serum lists Centella asiatica extract, alongside sodium hyaluronate, hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid and hyaluronic acid. These are three INCI forms; the list does not specify three molecular weights. The studies below explore hydration and appearance using other preparations.

For persistent redness, acne, eczema or rosacea, use the skin-condition guidance to help discuss formula fit with your clinician.

HUMAN INGREDIENT STUDY

Centella asiatica extract

Ratz-Łyko et al., 2016: 25 healthy volunteers; 2.5% and 5% extract creams/gels, twice daily on forearms for four weeks.

The tested preparations improved hydration and reduced the response to experimentally induced irritation.

How to read it: Different extract preparations and concentrations; healthy forearm skin, not an acne or rosacea treatment trial and not a trial of this sunscreen.

Read the study (opens in a new tab)

HUMAN INGREDIENT STUDY

Hyaluronic acid

Pavicic et al., 2011: 76 women; 0.1% HA creams at five molecular weights, twice daily for 60 days, compared with vehicle around the other eye.

The tested HA creams improved hydration and elasticity; two lower molecular weights also improved wrinkle-depth measurements at day 60.

How to read it: Specific creams and known molecular weights; the sunscreen’s HA concentrations and molecular weights are not verified by this study.

Read the study (opens in a new tab)

HUMAN INGREDIENT STUDY

Niacinamide

Bissett et al., 2005: 50 women; randomized, double-blind split-face comparison of 5% niacinamide and vehicle, twice daily for 12 weeks.

The tested preparation improved several measures of photoaged skin appearance, including fine lines and uneven pigmentation.

How to read it: A 5% test preparation, not this sunscreen; its niacinamide concentration and equivalent outcomes cannot be inferred.

Read the study (opens in a new tab)

HUMAN INGREDIENT STUDY

Glycerin

Lodén and Wessman, 2001: 17 healthy volunteers; bilateral, double-blind comparison of 20% glycerin cream and vehicle for ten days.

Hydration measurements increased with the glycerin cream.

How to read it: The study did not show improved water loss or resistance to detergent irritation. Different concentration and finished formula.

Read the study (opens in a new tab)

HUMAN INGREDIENT STUDY

Adenosine

Abella, 2006: 126 women aged 45–65; controlled study of a 0.1% adenosine cream or 1% dissolvable film; assessments at three and eight weeks.

The tested adenosine preparations improved measurements of facial lines.

How to read it: Different dose and delivery systems, not this sunscreen; not evidence of a seven-day sunscreen result.

PHYSICIAN REVIEW · SECONDARY REPORTING

Doctor Anne

Her November 2022 review reports Korea Dermatology Research Institute results of SPF 50.5 ± 7.1 and PFA 16.56 ± 2.69. PFA describes UVA protection, a separate measure from SPF.

The review discloses a gifted product and affiliate links. Its accompanying video was sponsored by the original brand. This is commentary, rather than a new clinical trial.

A separate hydrating serum with Centella Asiatica Leaf Water, five forms of hyaluronic acid and Ceramide NP. It has no SPF label. Its ingredient list cannot stand in for the sunscreen’s formula or protection testing.
