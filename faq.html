Choosing your essential

Which product should I start with?+
Daily Sun Serum 50ml is the SPF50+ PA++++ protection step. Cloud Mist 120ml is an optional hydration step. Compare them on the daily pairing page or use the routine finder.
Will they suit oily, combination, dry or sensitive skin?+
Consider finish preferences as well as ingredients. Both are described with a dewy finish; neither promises a matte result or suitability for every person. Our skin guide includes ingredient references and guidance on introducing new skincare.
Are these suitable for acne-prone skin?+
We do not establish a non-comedogenic guarantee from a lightweight texture or one customer’s review. Check ingredients you already avoid and introduce a new product carefully.
Will the sunscreen leave a white cast or pill?+
The described finish is without white cast, and selected customer excerpts discuss their experience. Appearance and pilling can vary with the amount, skin and other products used. Read the Sun Serum questions.
Is the sunscreen water-resistant?+
We have not verified a water-resistance rating for this exact version. For swimming or heavy sweating, use a sunscreen with a water-resistance claim on its label and follow the directions.
Can I see genuine customer experiences?+
Our Reviews page contains dated excerpts supplied from the linked Shopee listings, with source links. Individual experiences are not guaranteed results.

Using your daily edit

How do I apply sunscreen and how much should I use?+
Apply evenly to exposed skin before going outside and follow the label. AAD guidance suggests at least a teaspoon for the face and reapplication every two hours outdoors. See the application guide and AAD directions (opens in a new tab).
Where does sunscreen fit with moisturiser and makeup?+
Finish basic skincare with sunscreen, let it settle and then apply makeup gently. Keep sunscreen reapplication in mind during outdoor exposure.
How do I use Cloud Mist?+
Hold 20–30cm from the face, close your eyes and spray once or twice, following the current packaging directions. Allow it to absorb.
Does Cloud Mist set makeup or replace sunscreen?+
Cloud Mist offers hydration and a dewy feel. It does not promise longer makeup wear, has no stated SPF and does not replace sunscreen reapplication.
Can I use these alongside retinol, vitamin C or a prescription?+
Check the directions for each product. Our routine finder does not assess ingredient interactions or prescriptions; ask a dermatologist or pharmacist if you have a compatibility concern.
Will these reverse ageing or produce results in seven days?+
We do not promise age reversal, lifespan extension or a guaranteed seven-day transformation. The expectations guide explains texture, finish and routine-building.

Ingredients, storage & product care

Where can I read the full ingredients and sensitivity guidance?+
Open the ingredient references. Check the current packaging for the product and market you purchase, particularly if you have a known allergy.
How long will a bottle last?+
Daily Sun Serum contains 50ml; Cloud Mist contains 120ml. Use frequency and amount vary, so we do not promise a fixed number of days. Use the amount needed for your skincare directions.
How do I check expiry, period after opening and storage?+
Read the bottle or box and ask the seller about the current batch before purchase. Keep sunscreen away from excessive heat and direct sun. See product care.
Can I order a sample or travel size?+
Aureveil’s linked essentials are Sun Serum 50ml and Cloud Mist 120ml. Ask the seller about current samples or other sizes; do not assume that a sample is included.
