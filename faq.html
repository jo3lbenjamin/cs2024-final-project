The products

What is the main product in the Aureveil edit?+
Aureveil Daily Sun Serum SPF50+ PA++++ 50ml. Cloud Mist 120ml is our optional supporting hydration product.
Does the mist replace sunscreen?+
No. Cloud Mist is a hydrating facial mist with no stated SPF. Use labelled sun protection and follow its directions.
Can I call Cloud Mist a makeup setting spray?+
Aureveil does not. Cloud Mist supports hydration, a refreshing feel and a dewy finish. It is not a makeup-fixing spray and does not promise longer wear.
Will these products reverse ageing?+
We make no age-reversal or lifespan-extension claims. Our focus is daily sun protection, hydration and comfortable skincare.
Where can I check ingredients?+
Visit our ingredients guide for an introduction to common ingredient roles. Always read the packaging on the product you receive for the current formula and directions.
