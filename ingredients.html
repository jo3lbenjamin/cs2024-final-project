No ingredient buzzword needs to become another thing to worry about. Here is a clear introduction to ingredient roles in everyday skincare.

The shared thread

Centella meets
hydration.

Centella asiatica and hyaluronic acid are familiar ingredients in moisturizing skincare. They offer different roles in a simple hydration routine.

Ingredient presence alone does not tell us how a finished product will feel on every person. Texture, the full formula and your individual skin all matter.

01

IN BOTH PRODUCTS

Centella asiatica

A botanical ingredient commonly used in soothing, moisturizing skincare. An ingredient story is a guide to its role, rather than a promise of wrinkle reversal.

02

IN BOTH PRODUCTS

Hyaluronic acid

A humectant ingredient used to support hydration. It helps attract water and supports a comfortable, moisturized feel.

03

IN THE CLOUD MIST

Green tea water & xylitol

Green tea water is used in moisturizing skincare, while xylitol can contribute to a refreshing feel. Hydration ingredients do not provide an SPF rating.

04

IN THE DAILY SUN SERUM

UV filters

UV filters are the sunscreen ingredients that protect against ultraviolet radiation. Follow the finished product’s labelled SPF rating and directions; ingredient names alone cannot tell you its protection level.

The label is your reference.

Formulas can vary by market and change over time. Read the ingredient list on the item you buy, especially if you avoid a particular ingredient. Check the current product details and packaging on Shopee before purchasing.
