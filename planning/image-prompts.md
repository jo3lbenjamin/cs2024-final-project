# Original campaign imagery

Generation mode: built-in image generation. No API key or fallback CLI was used.

Final project asset: `assets/images/lifestyle.webp` (encoded from the original generated PNG for web delivery).

Exact prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original editorial lifestyle photograph for an independent student skincare retailer website named Aureveil. Create one wide landscape photograph, approximately 3:2 composition. An East or Southeast Asian woman in her early thirties at home in a modern Singapore apartment, wearing a soft pale powder-blue cotton blouse, gently touching the side of her face and looking toward daylight with a calm, assured expression. Beautiful yet realistic skin with natural pores and a few subtle expression lines, no dramatic before/after or impossible flawlessness. Medium close-up portrait framed with breathing room and a softly blurred window, pale blue curtains, warm champagne-gold morning light. Premium beauty editorial photography with authentic textures, gentle shadows, understated luxury, airy and spacious. No skincare products, bottles, cosmetics, brand logos, writing, watermarks, typography or decorative UI. This is atmospheric campaign imagery, not a testimonial or proof of product results. Keep face and hands anatomically natural.
