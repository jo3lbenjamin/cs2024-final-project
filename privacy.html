Privacy notice for the Aureveil website. Updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

Aureveil is a skincare brand focused on daily protection and hydration. Purchases take place through external Shopee listings; orders and payments are handled there.

Routine finder

The form asks only about product and routine preferences. It requests no name, email, phone number, age, skin photograph or medical history. JavaScript processes selections within the current page. Answers are not sent to a server, saved to browser storage or used for profiling. Reloading or resetting the page clears the results.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies, use localStorage, run analytics or embed advertising pixels. Fonts, images and scripts are served locally from this site. We do not embed third-party videos or social widgets.

Hosting information

If published on GitHub Pages, GitHub may log technical information including visitor IP addresses for security purposes. This is separate from Aureveil’s browser-only form. See GitHub Pages data collection information and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

External websites

Purchase buttons open existing Shopee product listings. We do not send routine-finder selections with those links. Shopee, its sellers and other external websites handle information under their own policies. Any purchasing or account interaction happens on those external sites.

PDPA approach

Our approach is to minimise data use: the site has no personal-data collection form or backend, and its routine finder does not persist answers. If we introduce email capture or analytics, we will update this privacy notice and explain the collection, consent, retention and access process.

Using the site

Do not enter personal or sensitive information in the routine finder. It is designed for general product preferences only. For order support, use the seller’s contact options on the relevant Shopee listing. This notice applies to the current Aureveil website.
