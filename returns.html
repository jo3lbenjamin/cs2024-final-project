Before and after buying

The details
that matter.

Can I return opened skincare or change my mind?+
Do not assume that opened or used skincare qualifies for a change-of-mind return. Read the item’s eligibility conditions before opening, and ask the seller or Shopee if they are unclear. An issue such as a wrong or damaged item should be raised through the relevant order-support route.
What if an item is damaged, wrong or expired?+
Open the relevant Shopee order and raise the matching issue promptly, including requested evidence. Read how to raise a request (opens in a new tab) and check supporting evidence (opens in a new tab).
How long do I have to request a return?+
Shopee’s published policy describes a 15-day request period for received items, with conditions and a start date that depends on delivery method. Check your specific order’s deadline and act promptly. Approved returns may have separate deadlines to choose a method and send the parcel.
Who pays for return shipping?+
It depends on the approved reason and applicable policy. Review the shipping arrangement shown in the case before sending a parcel; do not assume that every return is free.
What if my skin reacts?+
Stop using a product that irritates your skin and seek appropriate medical advice if needed. Contact the seller or Shopee about the purchase; a skin reaction does not automatically establish refund eligibility.
Can Aureveil process my Shopee refund?+
Refund requests and order evidence are handled through Shopee. Use that order’s support options so the seller and platform can identify the purchase.

Introduce new skincare thoughtfully

Our 14-day sample-first policy.

  • Request a return within 14 days of delivery.
  • The full-size item must remain unopened, unused and sealed, with its original packaging.
  • Return included reusable gifts and unused samples. Used discovery sachets do not need to be returned.
  • For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays return postage. Original delivery charges are excluded from the product refund.
  • Request return instructions through your order’s seller chat before posting anything. Refunds are handled through the original order.

Damaged, incorrect, missing or expired items follow the relevant order-support route and applicable platform policy.
