Shipping depends on the selected order and address. Check the fee and any voucher minimum spend or cap in the order total. Free delivery is only included when your checkout shows it.

Good to know

Delivery questions.
Clearer answers.

Is this dispatched from Singapore?+
The linked Sun Serum and Cloud Mist listings currently show Korea as the dispatch origin, checked on 27 October 2026. Confirm Ships From and the delivery estimate for your selected variation and address before ordering.
Can you guarantee a delivery day?+
Use the estimate shown for your actual Shopee order. Customs, fulfilment and the carrier can affect arrival. For a time-sensitive order, ask the seller before paying.
Where can I find my tracking number?+
Sign in to the Shopee account used for the order and open its delivery details. Use the available seller chat or Shopee Help options if the tracking information needs clarification.
Will a Christmas gift arrive in time?+
Check the current arrival estimate for your recipient’s address before ordering. Ask the seller about a gift deadline, wrapping or a gift note; those extras are not automatically included.
