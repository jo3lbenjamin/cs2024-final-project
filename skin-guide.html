A little awareness goes a long way

Know what
you are choosing.

Make your own ingredient preferences part of the decision.

01

Read the whole label.

If you have a known allergy, check the full formula with a dermatologist. A short list of highlighted ingredients cannot establish that a product is safe for you.

02

Check familiar sensitivities.

Sun Serum’s reference list includes wheat sprout and birch extracts. Cloud Mist’s includes rose flower water and a ferment filtrate. Review these names if they are ingredients you already avoid; they are not a diagnosis of an allergy.

03

Introduce it thoughtfully.

AAD guidance recommends testing a new product on a small area, twice daily for 7–10 days, following its usual directions. Stop using it if a reaction develops.
