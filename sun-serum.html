A few useful answers

Get to know your SPF.

What do SPF50+ and PA++++ refer to?+
SPF describes protection against sunburn, primarily associated with UVB. PA is a UVA-protection rating. The Daily Sun Serum is presented with an SPF50+ PA++++ rating. Always follow the label on the item you purchase.
Can I wear makeup after sunscreen?+
Use sunscreen as the last step of skincare, let it settle, then apply makeup gently. Follow the product label for quantity and reapplication.

Before you choose

Your questions.
A clearer next step.

Will it suit oily or acne-prone skin?+
This sunscreen has a hydrating, dewy finish. If you prefer a matte feel, consider that preference before choosing. A lightweight description is not a non-comedogenic guarantee; review the ingredients and introduce new skincare carefully.
Will it pill or leave a white cast?+
The described finish is lightweight and without white cast, and our selected reviews discuss that experience. Pilling and appearance can vary with skin, amount and other products. Let skincare settle, apply sunscreen evenly, then apply makeup gently.
Is it water-resistant for swimming or exercise?+
We have not verified a water-resistance rating for this exact version. For swimming or heavy sweating, choose a sunscreen with a water-resistance claim on its label and follow its reapplication directions. SPF alone does not establish water resistance.
How much sunscreen should I use?+
Cover exposed skin evenly and follow your product label. General AAD guidance recommends at least a teaspoon for the face, about the length of the index and middle fingers. Apply before going outside and reapply at least every two hours outdoors. Read the AAD application guide (opens in a new tab).
How long will one bottle last?+
The 50ml bottle’s lifespan depends on the amount and frequency you use. Reapplication and the areas you cover also matter; use enough for protection rather than stretching a bottle.
How do I check expiry and storage?+
Read the expiry date and any open-jar period-after-opening symbol on the bottle or box. Ask the seller for the current batch details before ordering. Keep sunscreen away from excessive heat and direct sunlight. Explore product care.
How do delivery and returns work?+
Your order is placed on Shopee. Check the delivery estimate and fees for your address before payment, and read the applicable return conditions. Our shipping guide and returns guide explain where to find them.
